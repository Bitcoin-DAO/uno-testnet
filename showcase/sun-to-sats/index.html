Bitcoin is money you can hold yourself, with no bank in between. "Sats" are its smallest unit, the way cents are to a euro.

Sun to Sats is a group of ten people who share a solar site. The panels power a Bitcoin miner, and what it earns goes into one shared treasury. Every week the members vote to pay that week's earnings out to each member's own wallet, keeping a share back as a reserve.

There is no company behind it. Sun to Sats is a DAO: the members decide by vote, five of them hold the treasury's keys together, and any three must sign before a single sat moves. Nobody can spend alone, and every payment is public.

One thing to know before you start: once bitcoin moves, it cannot be undone. Your recovery words are the only thing that controls your wallet. Share your address freely; never share those words.

How the money moves

The pool pays the miner's earnings to the treasury. Each week a distribution proposal splits them: —. Members vote for seven days; then three of the five council members sign, and the payment goes out in one transaction.

How to join

Joining happens in person, and the members vote on it. There is no form to fill in.

  1. Meet the members

    Come to a meeting. A member who knows you can sponsor you.

  2. Cut your key

    At a key-cutting session you make your own Bitcoin wallet by hand: dice, a steel plate, and a signing device. Nobody else ever sees your recovery words.

  3. Join the DAO

    In the same session you create your DAO identity on your phone and a council member verifies you. The members then vote you in.

  4. Get paid weekly

    From the next distribution, your share goes straight to your wallet.
